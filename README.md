Cybersecurity student at **Mansoura University**, Egypt — living at the intersection of offensive security, systems thinking, and building things that either break other things or protect them.

### CAT Reloaded

<sub>Computer Assistance Team — Mansoura University</sub>

<br><br>

CAT Reloaded is a non-profit technical community founded in 1996 at the Faculty of Engineering, Mansoura University, and relaunched to carry Computer Science and IT education forward for a new generation of students. The team runs technical circles spanning cybersecurity, web and mobile development, data science, and design — through workshops, talks, and hackathons, including the annual "Hello, World!" recruitment event.

I hold a supervisory role in the **PR & Fundraising Circle**, working on partnerships, sponsorship outreach, and the operations that keep the community running.
